The Seal Cut into Seven

Difficulty ★★☆about 5 minLogic PuzzleObservationChecking the conditions
To open the box left by a missing grandfather, a round seal plate must be cut into seven pieces with three cuts. Pick the right pattern from four diagrams.

Case Record

In the study of a grandfather who has gone missing, a locked box was found. It seems to hold the records of the last case he was investigating.

On the lid is a round seal plate and a device that cuts the plate in straight lines. The position and direction of each cut can be changed, but the device can be operated only three more times.

A note from grandfather was attached to the box.

"Cut the circle into seven pieces, and the box will open. Each cut must run straight from edge to edge. Do not move or stack the pieces once cut."

Your question

Which cutting pattern splits the seal plate into seven pieces with three cuts? Look at the diagrams and deduce it.

The Truth

Cut pattern 4

The answer is Cut pattern 4.

If the three cuts cross one another at three different points, a small triangle forms in the middle and six pieces surround it: seven pieces in all.

If all three cuts meet at a single point, you get only six pieces. Pattern 1 (three parallel lines) gives four pieces, pattern 2 (three lines through one point) gives six, and pattern 3 (two parallel lines crossed by a third) gives six.

The key was to move the crossing points apart so that every pair of cuts crosses inside the circle.

Key Points

  • The more the cuts cross, the more pieces there are
  • Three cuts through one point add fewer pieces
  • Move the crossing points apart

Why people get fooled

You feel drawn to a neat arrangement where all three cuts meet at one point, and never try shifting the crossing points.
