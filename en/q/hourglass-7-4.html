Nine Minutes from Two Hourglasses

Difficulty ★★☆about 6 minLogic PuzzleHourglassProcedure
With a 7-minute and a 4-minute hourglass, how do you measure 9 minutes?

Case Record

You need to time 9 minutes. You have a 7-minute hourglass and a 4-minute hourglass. You can flip either at any moment.

You follow this procedure:

  1. Flip both at the same time to start.
  2. When the 4-minute one runs out, flip it right away.
  3. When the 7-minute one runs out, flip it right away.
  4. ...

What is step 4?

The Truth

At 8 minutes, when the 4-minute one runs out, flip the 7-minute one; when it runs out, 9 minutes have passed

The answer is: at 8 minutes, when the 4-minute one runs out, flip the 7-minute one; when it runs out, 9 minutes have passed.

At 0 start. At 4 the small one runs out; flip it. At 7 the big one runs out; flip it (1 minute of sand is still in the top of the small one). At 8 the small one runs out. The 7-minute glass flipped at 7 has run for 1 minute, so 1 minute of sand is in the bottom. Flip it at 8 and that 1 minute of sand runs through, finishing at 9 minutes.

Key Points

  • The sand already fallen is the time you can measure next
  • Minute 8 is when the 4-minute glass runs out again
  • Use the leftover 1 minute to reach 9

Why people get fooled

We think a glass must run fully before being flipped.
