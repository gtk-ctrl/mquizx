The Magic Square Lock

Difficulty ★☆☆about 4 minLogic PuzzleMagic squaresEqual sums
A 3x3 grid uses 1 to 9. Every row, column and diagonal sums to 15. What is the centre?

Case Record

A safe door has a 3 x 3 grid of nine squares. Each square holds one of the numbers 1 to 9, used once. The door opens when every row, every column and both diagonals add up to 15.

You know the top row is 2, 7, 6, and the bottom-left square is 4.

What number is in the centre?

5

The answer is: 5.

The top-right square (6), the centre and the bottom-left square (4) lie on one diagonal. 6 + centre + 4 = 15, so the centre is 5. The completed square is 2 7 6 / 9 5 1 / 4 3 8. The centre of a 3 x 3 magic square is always 5.

Key Points

  • Find a line you can complete first
  • One unknown on a line of three is easy
  • Use sum = 15

Why people get fooled

Looking only at the top row and left column, you can miss the diagonal clue.
