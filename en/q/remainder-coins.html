Counting Coins by Remainders

Difficulty ★★☆about 6 minLogic PuzzleRemaindersNarrowing down
Fewer than 100 coins. Remainders: 2 when grouped by 3, 3 by 5, 2 by 7.

Case Record

In a thief's hideout, police found a bag of gold coins. The count is unknown, but the thief's notebook has these notes:

  • Dividing into groups of 3 leaves 2
  • Dividing into groups of 5 leaves 3
  • Dividing into groups of 7 leaves 2

The bag holds fewer than 100 coins.

How many coins were in the bag?

The Truth

Share my result

23

The answer is: 23.

Numbers that leave 2 when divided by 3 and 3 when divided by 5 are 8, 23, 38, 53, 68, 83, 98 (they come every 15). Only 23 also leaves 2 when divided by 7 (23 = 7 x 3 + 2). The others leave 1, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 0.

Key Points

  • Use two conditions first to build a short list
  • Use the third condition to pick one
  • Do not forget the range limit

Why people get fooled

Trying to satisfy all three conditions at once loses track of the clues.
