The Gem That Changed Weight

Difficulty ★☆☆about 3 minTheft CaseDensityWeight
A diamond on display was weighed before and after the exhibit. It is the same size, yet the weight differs.

Case Record

A diamond was swapped out of a museum display case. The stone had been cut into a cube measuring 1 cm on a side, so its volume is 1 cubic centimeter. The weight measured before the exhibit was 3.5 grams.

After the exhibit, the stone looked the same in shape and size. But when it was weighed again, it came to only 2.5 grams.

The Truth

It was swapped for a fake made of glass

The answer is that it was swapped for a fake made of glass.

If the size is the same (1 cubic centimeter) but the weight differs, the material differs. Diamond is about 3.5 grams and glass is about 2.5 grams. The stone weighing 2.5 grams after the exhibit matches the density of glass exactly.

If it had been ground down to make it lighter, its volume would have shrunk. When the size stays the same and only the weight is lighter, the material has been switched.

Key Points

  • If the volume is the same and the weight differs, the material differs
  • Compare density (weight per cubic centimeter)
  • Do not decide something is real just because it looks the same

Why people get fooled

Because the shape and size look the same, you find it hard to imagine a switch.
